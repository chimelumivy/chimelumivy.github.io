What if data could tell a story that numbers alone couldn’t?

Hi!, I'm Ivy

My curiosity about this began in medical school, while collecting data for a research project on healthcare workers’ attitudes towards eye donation. I found myself wondering what the numbers would reveal: What did healthcare workers really know? How did their attitudes shape their practice? And what insights could the data uncover? That curiosity eventually led me to pursue formal training in data analytics during my medical internship.

Today, I use Excel SQL, Power BI, and Python(basic) to transform raw data into meaningful insights. What sets me apart is the perspective I bring beyond the numbers, with a background in medicine, business operations, and volunteer leadership, I bring analytical thinking, creativity, problem-solving, and project coordination skills to every challenge.

I believe every dataset has a story to tell. My goal is to uncover it, make sense of the numbers, and turn insights into decisions that matter. I’m passionate about uncovering insights that solve real-world problems, improve processes, and support smarter decisions across industries.
